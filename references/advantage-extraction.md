# Advantage extraction · 吸

For EACH candidate advantage record:
- Dimension / competitor / exact relevant URL.
- What users do and why it works (mechanism), with evidence level and date.
- Target user fit, usability and accessibility trade-offs.
- Costs: engineering, maintenance, localization, legal/IP, performance and privacy.
- Transfer decision: **Adopt** (principle only), **Adapt**, **Experiment**, or **Reject**.
- Our original synthesis: how to deliver the benefit with our own voice.
- Verification metric and regression risk.

Distinguish facts (observed interactions), hypotheses (expected value), and proposals (not built). Resist copying logos, text, licensed imagery, proprietary implementation, or dark patterns. Competing implementation is inspiration, not authorization to reproduce protected materials.

Prioritize evidence-backed improvements to the user's core job. For machine validation use optional `advantages`/`roadmap` in [audit JSON](audit-format.md). Every adopted idea must connect to applicability, a concrete change and a success criterion. Use the CSV in `assets/advantage-matrix.csv` to structure comparisons.
